Harumung Privacy Policy

Effective: October 20, 2026

90ms (“we”) respects the privacy of everyone who uses the Android app Harumung (the “app”). This policy explains what information the app handles, where it stays and how it is used.

1. Information we collect

The app has no sign-up or login, and we run no server that receives your information. Your records, special days, memos, daily reviews, pet and garden data and settings are stored only on your device and are never sent to us. We do not view, keep or share them.

2. Ads and third-party services (Google AdMob)

The app is free and shows ads from Google AdMob. To serve and measure ads, Google may collect and process:

  • your advertising ID and device and app information (model, OS version, language and similar)
  • your IP address and the approximate location derived from it
  • how you interact with ads, such as views and taps

Users in the European Economic Area, the UK, Switzerland and certain US states are asked for their choice through Google’s User Messaging Platform before any ad is requested. You can change that choice at any time in My records › Settings › Ad privacy choices. You can also reset or delete your advertising ID in your device settings.

See the Google Privacy Policy and How Google uses information from sites or apps that use its services.

3. Device permissions

  • Calendar (optional): when you turn on external calendar sync, the app shows events from your device calendars and saves the records you choose to the calendar you pick. This happens on your device and nothing is sent to us. If that calendar syncs with an account such as Google Calendar, that service’s policy applies to the sync.
  • Notifications, exact alarms and start at boot: to deliver the record and review reminders you set on time and keep them scheduled after a restart.
  • Full-screen notifications (optional): used only if you turn on the pet reminder screen.
  • Internet and network state: to load ads and check your ad consent status.
  • App lock (optional): uses your device’s screen lock and biometric prompt. The app never accesses or stores fingerprints, face data or other biometric information.

4. Backups

  • File backup: a backup file you export is saved where you choose, and you control that file.
  • Android backup: if Google backup is on for your device, the app’s data is included in your Google Account backup and kept under Google’s policies. We cannot access it.

5. Retention and deletion

App data is removed from your device when you delete it in the app or uninstall the app. Because we keep no user data, there is nothing for us to delete. Information Google processes for ads is retained under Google’s policies.

6. Sharing

We do not share your personal information with third parties or have anyone process it for us. Google collects and processes information directly through the ad SDK in the app, as described in section 2, and it may be transferred to Google’s servers outside your country.

7. Children

The app is not directed to children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect information from them.

8. Your rights

All app data stays on your device, so you can view, edit and delete it directly in the app. You can change your ad choices as described in section 2. If you have questions, contact us below.

10. Changes

If this policy changes, we will post the new version on this page with its effective date. If the app adds features such as accounts or server backup that change what is collected, we will update this policy before those features are offered.
